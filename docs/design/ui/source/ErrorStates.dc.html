egui mapping: error notice
widgets::error_notice is one Frame: tint_danger fill (tint_warning for a guided condition), status glyph and title in the tone colour, the envelope message in text_primary, the diagnostic id in monospace, then the controls ErrorAction implies.
It renders the envelope; it never classifies or rewrites the failure.
Startup states, as built
Before the shell paints: a centred Spinner with "Loading your library…", or "Plaudon could not start" with Try again. Both use ui.vertical_centered in a CentralPanel, no rail.
Offline guidance, as built
job_presentation::error_guidance maps LocalOnlyMode to "You are offline" with Review Local-only Mode, StorageFull to "Not enough space", BudgetExceeded to "Provider credits exhausted", OutcomeUnknown to "Cloud request outcome unknown".
The body sentence on the offline panel is the engines section's own copy.
Unavailable, not empty
RadioState::UnavailableInBuild and a denied radio each get their own sentence; neither looks like an empty scan. A missing audio device hides the slider and says so in the backend's words.
Disabled controls draw at 0.5 alpha and stay in the AccessKit tree with their names.
