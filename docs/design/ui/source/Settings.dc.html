Privacy & network

Recordings, transcripts and summaries stay on this computer. Cloud AI is opt-in: Plaudon sends nothing to a provider until you choose one and consent for that purpose.

✓ Local only

Voice profiles

Voice matching compares a voiceprint computed on this device against the people you have enrolled. A voiceprint is biometric data, it never leaves this machine, and matching stays off until you turn it on. A match is only ever a suggestion you confirm.

– No speaker-embedding model in this build

One ScrollArea of sections, each settings::section() drawn as a Frame (bg_surface, radius.large). Appearance and AI key sit side by side via ui.columns(2) when the main width is over 820 pt, stacked otherwise.

Local-only mode, as built
egui::Checkbox labelled "Local-only mode (refuse all outbound network access)". Ticking it sends set_local_only_mode with confirmed: true; the toggle is the confirmation. It is disabled while the request is in flight.
The badge and the "Enforced policy" lines render only after the gate answers. Before that, the safe reading is on, and no policy sentence is composed.
Appearance, as built
Two radio groups (radio_value) for Follow the system, Light, Dark and Standard, High contrast. ThemeState::apply installs both styles with set_style_of; the choice persists in shell-state.json until set_appearance exists.
AI key, as built
TextEdit::singleline(..).password(true), handed over once and cleared. Only presence is shown: "Key saved in the system keychain" or "No key saved". Remove asks first: "Remove the OpenRouter key?" with its disclosure.
